Get in touch

illume reach is built for journal editors who want a consistent, professional presence on LinkedIn and Bluesky — without writing a single post manually. Connect your journal data, and reach handles everything: scoring content by impact, generating platform-native posts using AI, and publishing on a schedule you control.

What it does

Smart scoring

Every article and author is scored across citations, h-index, and engagement signals — ranked into four tiers from Contributing to Elite. Your most impactful research always gets promoted first.

AI post generation

Claude AI reads your abstracts and writes compelling, platform-native posts for LinkedIn and Bluesky. Each post is tailored to the article's findings and audience. OpenAI provides a seamless fallback.

Five post types

Article Influence, Author Influence, Author Spotlight, Recent Publications, and Special Issue. Mix and match in reusable weekly programs to keep your feed varied and engaging.

Branded image tiles

Every post is paired with an automatically generated image — branded with your journal's identity, showing the article title, citation count, and key metrics. No design work required.

LinkedIn & Bluesky publishing

Publish directly to both platforms from one place. Connect Google Sheets via Zapier for additional workflow automation. Posts go live on your schedule, completely hands-free.

Editorial dashboard

Review posts before they go live, edit copy, exclude specific articles or authors, and manage your weekly program — from a clean dashboard built for editorial teams.

How it works

01

Connect your journal

Link your Scopus API and journal database. reach pulls your articles and author profiles automatically and keeps them current.

02

Score & rank

reach analyses citation data, h-index scores, and engagement signals to rank every article and author by influence.

03

Generate & review

AI writes platform-ready posts and creates branded image tiles. Review everything in your dashboard before anything goes live.

04

Publish on schedule

Approve posts individually or enable auto-approval. Your weekly program runs automatically to LinkedIn and Bluesky.
